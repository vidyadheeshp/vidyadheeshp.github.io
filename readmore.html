What I work with

  • Android development in Android Studio, and WordPress for sites that need to be handed over to someone who does not write code.
  • Still, after eight years, more interested in the next thing than in the last one. Most of the list above started as something I did not know.

Beyond the department

I read Indian philosophy — particularly Dvaita epistemology and tarka, the classical logic tradition. It is not a hobby kept at arm's length from the day job: Pāṇini's Aṣṭādhyāyī is a formal grammar with rule ordering and metarules, written twenty-five centuries before anyone spoke of parsers, and reading it that way is what led to Vāk, a programming language whose type system encodes Pāṇini's kāraka roles.

In August 2025 I helped establish the Centre for Sanskrit Learning at KLS Gogte Institute of Technology, in collaboration with Central Sanskrit University, Delhi.
